## Учебна илюстрация: управление с глава

`head-mouse-in-use.png` е създадена с вградения инструмент imagegen. Представя измислена учебна сцена, а не документална снимка на конкретен продукт. Пунктираната линия е схематична.

Промпт:

Create one photorealistic educational illustration for a Bulgarian high-school lesson about a head-controlled computer mouse. Landscape composition 3:2. Show a generic adult seated at a desk, in a clear three-quarter side profile, using a desktop monitor without touching a keyboard or mouse, hands resting naturally in lap. A small circular silver reflective sticker is clearly visible on the user’s forehead. A small black infrared head-tracking camera is attached to the TOP CENTER of the monitor bezel, facing the user’s forehead. The monitor screen is visible from our viewing angle and displays a simple clean desktop with a LARGE easily visible arrow cursor. Frame the person’s face, reflective dot, camera and screen prominently so their spatial relationship is obvious. The person is on the left facing the monitor on the right; realistic natural anatomy, credible desk geometry and light. Add a subtle thin dashed BLUE instructional line visually connecting the tracking camera to the reflective forehead dot; this is a diagram overlay, not a real visible laser beam. No text, no brand logos, no medical equipment, no headset, no eye-tracking glasses. The technology tracks the forehead marker and head motion, not eye gaze. This should look like a careful photographic classroom explainer, not an advertisement. It is a generic explanatory setup, not a purported documentary photograph of a particular branded product.
